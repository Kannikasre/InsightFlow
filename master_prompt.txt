# 1. PRODUCT VISION

Businesses receive large volumes of customer feedback through reviews, surveys, and support messages. They struggle to identify recurring problems, understand customer sentiment, recognize emerging complaints, and decide what to fix first.

InsightFlow solves this problem by converting raw customer feedback into meaningful, evidence-backed business decisions.

The complete user journey should be:

**Import feedback → Analyze data → Detect customer problems → Investigate evidence → Prioritize issues → Generate recommendations → Track fixes → Measure changes → Export reports.**

The product should feel like an intelligent investigation workspace for business decisions, rather than an ordinary analytics dashboard.

Its central differentiator is **evidence-first intelligence**: every major insight should connect to the feedback records and calculations that support it.
